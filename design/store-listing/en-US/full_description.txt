Your camera roll already remembers every trip. Wend finds them.

Wend reads when and where each photo and video was taken, and gathers them into trips on its own. No albums to make, no tagging, no planning. Just open the app and watch your photos come together.

◆ YOUR TRIPS, FOUND FOR YOU
Wend looks through your whole library and groups photos into trips: a week abroad, a weekend by the sea, a day out in the neighborhood. Each trip gets its own color on a living globe, so you can see your travels at a glance.

◆ RELIVE EACH TRIP IN ORDER
Open a trip and read it the way it happened, day by day and place by place. As you scroll, a pin walks your route on a paper-style map.

◆ PICK YOUR BEST SHOTS
Took five almost-identical photos at the same spot? Wend lines them up so you can keep the best one. Your picks become the cover of each trip.

◆ CLEAN UP WITH CONFIDENCE
Wend suggests blurry, too-dark and near-duplicate photos you may not need. Nothing is removed until you choose. Photos go to your phone's trash only, so you can restore them for 30 days.

◆ YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Missed a few photos? Add them to any trip, or make a new trip yourself. Your changes are kept even when Wend looks for trips again.

◆ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.
• Wend never deletes a photo without your say.
• Clearing Wend's data never touches the photos on your device.

Tip: Wend needs photos with location info. Turn on location tags in your camera app so your next trip shows up too.

Wend: for wherever you wander.
